# 2025 Day 3: Lobby

## Puzzle
https://adventofcode.com/2025/day/3

## Part 1
- Aim: determine the sum of the highest joltages from the battery banks.
    - for each bank use the highest joltage that can be made by turning on two batteries.
    - the order of the batteries within the bank cannot be changed.
        
## Part 2
- Aim: as part 1, but now the 12 batteries that result in the highest joltage should be turned on for each bank.
